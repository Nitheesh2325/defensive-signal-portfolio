# Code of Conduct

## Our aim

Defensive Signal is a place to learn and build together. We want everyone who
takes part, whatever their experience, background, or identity, to be treated
with respect and to feel safe asking questions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming you understand.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information without their
  explicit permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including in examples, test
  data, or screenshots.
- Using the project to demonstrate attacks against systems you are not
  authorised to test.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers using the contact method listed
on the repository's profile, or through a private security advisory if no
other private channel is available. Reports are handled confidentially and
reviewed promptly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behaviour breaks this code.
Decisions consider the impact on the people affected and the wider community.
